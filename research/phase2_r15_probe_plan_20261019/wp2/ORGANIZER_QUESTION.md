**Subject:** Challenge 2 — resubmitting a variant that differs in one documented constant

Dear TechArena Challenge 2 organisers,

Our model contains one physically motivated constant that the released data cannot settle: a correction to one reference charge that started from an unrelaxed state. Our documentation records two hypotheses, with and without this correction.

We would like to submit once a variant that differs from our current submission only in this constant. Depending on the public leaderboard, we would then keep it or return to our current model. Both versions and the reason for the variant would be disclosed in our README and report. No checkup-specific values would be used or derived.

1. Is this acceptable, given the rule that the hidden checkups must not be reconstructed? If not, we will not submit the variant.
2. Could you also confirm:
   - the submission deadline and its time zone;
   - whether the leaderboard is updated weekly (challenge description) or every two weeks (submission guide);
   - whether the leaderboard shows one score or several metrics, and with how many decimals;
   - the data budget of the reduced-budget run.
