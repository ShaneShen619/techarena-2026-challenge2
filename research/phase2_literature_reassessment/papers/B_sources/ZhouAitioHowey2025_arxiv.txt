                                           Learning Li-ion battery health and degradation modes from data with aging-aware circuit models
                                           Zihao Zhou, Antti Aitio, David Howey


                                              • An aging-aware equivalent circuit model is proposed for battery health estimation
                                              • It estimates capacity and resistance only from operational data without labels

                                              • Changes of open-circuit voltage are strongly reflected in resistance estimation
                                              • It may enable an in operando estimation of differential voltage curves

ARTICLE INFO                                        ABSTRACT
Keywords:                                           Non-invasive estimation of Li-ion battery state-of-health from operational data is valuable for battery
Battery                                             applications, but remains challenging. Pure model-based methods may suffer from inaccuracy and
Health                                              long-term instability of parameter estimates, whereas pure data-driven methods rely heavily on
Estimation                                          training data quality and quantity, causing lack of generality when extrapolating to unseen cases.
Gaussian process                                    We apply an aging-aware equivalent circuit model for health estimation, combining the flexibility
State space                                         of data-driven techniques within a model-based approach. A simplified electrical model with voltage
Equivalent circuit                                  source and resistor incorporates Gaussian process regression to learn capacity fade over time and also
                                                    the dependence of resistance on operating conditions and time. The approach was validated against
                                                    two datasets and shown to give accurate performance with less than 1% relative root mean square error
                                                    (RMSE) in capacity and less than 2% mean absolute percentage error (MAPE). Critically, we show
                                                    that changes from the open circuit voltage versus state-of-charge function will strongly influence the
                                                    learnt resistance. We use this feature to further estimate in operando differential voltage curves from
                                                    operational data.
