Supplementary data: Battery pack diagnostics for electric vehicles: Robustness of the state of health measurement and differential voltage analysis at the vehicle level

Beschreibung:
This datasets contains the measurement data recorded while performing extensive experiments using state of the art battery electric vehicles for battery diagnosis with differential voltage analysis. The dataset includes small current measurements at the vehicle level during AC charging.
