# Stage 1 — OCPs, electrode model and synthetic identifiability (no proxy metric)

> **Language:** English · [中文](#zh)

Protocol: [protocol.json](protocol.json) (SHA256 in [protocol.sha256](protocol.sha256), committed before 1c/1d were run). Code: [tabulate_ocp.py](tabulate_ocp.py), [../src/emodel.py](../src/emodel.py), [../src/epack.py](../src/epack.py), [fit_1c.py](fit_1c.py), [synthetic_1d.py](synthetic_1d.py).

## 1a — OCP sources

PyBaMM 26.10.1.0 (BSD-3) was used: the LFP OCP `LFP_ocp_Afshar2017` from the `Prada2013` set, with graphite `graphite_LGM50_ocp_Chen2020` (main) or `graphite_ocp_Ecker2015` (alternative). The tables are in [../ocp/](../ocp/) with [SOURCES.md](../ocp/SOURCES.md). None of these curves was measured on the pack's cells.

## 1b — Model

The model follows the prompt exactly: θ_n = θ_n,0 + q/Q_n, θ_p = θ_p,0 − q/Q_p, Q_Li constant, and V = U_p − U_n + R·I + h·sign(I).

The pack simulation conventions (start state defined by voltage, CCCV at 20.4 A / 14.0 V / 5.1 A, a 10.782 A program discharge with the midpoint step rule, and a C/20 root with c_pack = −0.003 V) were registered before use.

## 1c — Fits of the real anchor 1 ([outputs/S1_anchor1_fits.csv](outputs/S1_anchor1_fits.csv))

| Fit | RMS (mV) | Q_p | θ_p,0 | Residual RMS by region (mV): 2–10 / 10–50 / 50–80 / 80–90 / 90–100 Ah | lag-1 ρ | n_eff of 1701 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main (4 cells) | 10.2–11.1 | **200 (bound)** | 0.49 | 31–34 / 1.4–1.6 / 4.9–5.2 / 3.7–4.0 / 11–14 | 0.998 | 16–17 |
| alt graphite | 8.0–8.6 | **200 (bound)** | 0.49 | 8–10 / 5.3–5.6 / 11.5–12 / 3.0–3.4 / 7–11 | 0.998 | 8–9 |
| main + dV/dq | 10.2–11.0 | **200 (bound)** | 0.49 | as main | 0.998 | 16–17 |

**The literature OCPs do not represent these cells.**

- **The best fits are unphysical.** Every fit puts Q_p at its 200 Ah bound and θ_p,0 ≈ 0.49: the fit uses only the top half of the LFP and gets its top-of-charge rise from the LFP's delithiated end. A physically placed LFP window (θ_p from about 0.93 to 0.02, Q_p ≈ 106 Ah, found in a development fit with Q_p ≤ 115) costs more and drives h to its 0.2 V bound with a negative R.
- **The misfit is concentrated in the regions that matter.** It sits in the bottom onset (2–10 Ah) and the top (90–100 Ah), which carry the end-point information for Q_C20. In the graphite-step region (50–80 Ah), which carries β, the error is 5 mV (12 mV with the alternative graphite). Only the mid-plateau (10–50 Ah) fits to 1–2 mV.
- **Residuals are almost perfectly autocorrelated** (lag-1 ρ = 0.998). The effective sample size is about 8–17 points out of 1701 (rule 7), so any profile range on real data will be wide.

## 1d — Synthetic identifiability ([outputs/S1_synthetic_summary.json](outputs/S1_synthetic_summary.json))

There were 30 random states (LLI 0–8 Ah, LAM_NE and LAM_PE 0–5 %, start shift ±30 mV), generated by the model itself with registered noise (2 mV white + 2 mV AR(1) ρ 0.9 + ±3 mV offset).

| Quantity | Median absolute error |
|---|---|
| Q_C20 | **0.034 Ah** (pass ≤ 0.2) |
| β_E | **0.071** (pass ≤ 0.10) |
| Q_fc | 0.040 Ah |
| LLI | 0.17 Ah |

**1d passes.** When the OCPs are right, the model identifies Q_C20 and β_E well. Two further observations:

- β_E depends on the degradation mode. It is about 1.1–1.8 for LLI- or LAM_PE-dominated states and can turn negative for LAM_NE-dominated states. In 6 of the 30 states the program-step shift is below 0.5 Ah, so β is ill-conditioned there.
- The real-data problem (1c) is model mismatch, which the synthetic test cannot detect by design. Stages 2 and 3 decide whether the real fits can be used.

## Implementation notes

- The registered 144-start grid is searched in two phases: all starts with 60 function evaluations, then the best three refined to convergence (D1.1). On anchor 1 this returns the same optimum as the full search.
- The synthetic anchors are generated by the model's own CCCV at 10 s steps (CC 20.4 A, CV to 5.1 A), rather than by replaying anchor 1's exact current, because the profile must follow the degraded state (D1.2).

# 第 1 阶段——OCP、电极模型与合成数据可辨识性（不算代理指标）

> **语言：** [English](#en) · 中文

协议：[protocol.json](protocol.json)（SHA256 见 [protocol.sha256](protocol.sha256)，在运行 1c/1d 之前提交）。代码：[tabulate_ocp.py](tabulate_ocp.py)、[../src/emodel.py](../src/emodel.py)、[../src/epack.py](../src/epack.py)、[fit_1c.py](fit_1c.py)、[synthetic_1d.py](synthetic_1d.py)。

## 1a——OCP 来源

用的是 PyBaMM 26.10.1.0（BSD-3）：`Prada2013` 参数集中的 LFP OCP `LFP_ocp_Afshar2017`，配石墨 `graphite_LGM50_ocp_Chen2020`（主配置）或 `graphite_ocp_Ecker2015`（备选）。列表在 [../ocp/](../ocp/)，附 [SOURCES.md](../ocp/SOURCES.md)。这些曲线都不是在本电池组的电芯上测得的。

## 1b——模型

模型严格按 prompt 实现：θ_n = θ_n,0 + q/Q_n，θ_p = θ_p,0 − q/Q_p，Q_Li 为常数，V = U_p − U_n + R·I + h·sign(I)。

电池组模拟的约定（用电压定义起始状态；CCCV 为 20.4 A / 14.0 V / 5.1 A；程序放电 10.782 A，用中点台阶规则；C/20 求根时 c_pack = −0.003 V）都在使用前登记。

## 1c——真实锚点 1 的拟合（[outputs/S1_anchor1_fits.csv](outputs/S1_anchor1_fits.csv)）

| 拟合 | RMS（mV） | Q_p | θ_p,0 | 分区残差 RMS（mV）：2–10 / 10–50 / 50–80 / 80–90 / 90–100 Ah | 滞后 1 的 ρ | 1701 点中的 n_eff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 主配置（4 芯） | 10.2–11.1 | **200（上界）** | 0.49 | 31–34 / 1.4–1.6 / 4.9–5.2 / 3.7–4.0 / 11–14 | 0.998 | 16–17 |
| 备选石墨 | 8.0–8.6 | **200（上界）** | 0.49 | 8–10 / 5.3–5.6 / 11.5–12 / 3.0–3.4 / 7–11 | 0.998 | 8–9 |
| 主配置 + dV/dq | 10.2–11.0 | **200（上界）** | 0.49 | 同主配置 | 0.998 | 16–17 |

**文献 OCP 表示不了这些电芯。**

- **最优拟合不合物理。** 每个拟合都把 Q_p 推到 200 Ah 上界、θ_p,0 ≈ 0.49：拟合只用了 LFP 的上半段，用 LFP 的脱锂端来产生充电末端的陡升。物理上合理的 LFP 窗口（θ_p 约从 0.93 到 0.02，Q_p ≈ 106 Ah，是在 Q_p ≤ 115 的开发拟合中找到的）代价更高，还会把 h 推到 0.2 V 上界、R 变成负值。
- **失配集中在关键区域。** 它出现在底端起始段（2–10 Ah）和顶端（90–100 Ah），而这两处携带 Q_C20 所需的端点信息。在携带 β 信息的石墨台阶区（50–80 Ah），误差为 5 mV（备选石墨为 12 mV）。只有中段平台（10–50 Ah）拟合到 1–2 mV。
- **残差几乎完全自相关**（滞后 1 的 ρ = 0.998）。有效样本量只有 1701 个点中的约 8–17 个（规则 7），所以真实数据上的任何剖面范围都会很宽。

## 1d——合成数据可辨识性（[outputs/S1_synthetic_summary.json](outputs/S1_synthetic_summary.json)）

共 30 个随机状态（LLI 0–8 Ah、LAM_NE 和 LAM_PE 0–5 %、起点偏移 ±30 mV），由模型自身生成，加登记的噪声（2 mV 白噪声 + ρ 0.9 的 2 mV AR(1) + ±3 mV 偏差）。

| 量 | 绝对误差中位数 |
|---|---|
| Q_C20 | **0.034 Ah**（通过条件 ≤ 0.2） |
| β_E | **0.071**（通过条件 ≤ 0.10） |
| Q_fc | 0.040 Ah |
| LLI | 0.17 Ah |

**1d 通过。** OCP 正确时，模型能很好地辨识 Q_C20 和 β_E。另有两点观察：

- β_E 取决于退化模式。以 LLI 或 LAM_PE 为主的状态约为 1.1–1.8，以 LAM_NE 为主时可以变成负值。30 个状态中有 6 个的程序台阶偏移小于 0.5 Ah，这时 β 是病态的。
- 真实数据的问题（1c）是模型失配，而合成测试按设计检测不到它。真实拟合能否使用，由第 2、3 阶段决定。

## 实现说明

- 登记的 144 起点网格分两段搜索：所有起点先各跑 60 次函数评估，再把最好的三个细化到收敛（D1.1）。在锚点 1 上它与完整搜索得到同一个最优解。
- 合成锚点由模型自己的 10 s 步长 CCCV 生成（恒流 20.4 A，恒压到 5.1 A），而不是回放锚点 1 的实测电流，因为电流曲线必须随退化后的状态变化（D1.2）。
